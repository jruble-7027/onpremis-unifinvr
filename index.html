How to Bypass "Your connection is not private"

When clicking on an internal NVR IP address for the first time, your browser (Chrome or Edge) may display a red or gray security warning screen. This is normal for internal school devices because they use internal encryption certificates rather than external public web certificates.

1
Look for the "Advanced" or "More Details" button
At the bottom of the warning screen, click the button labeled "Advanced".
2
Click "Proceed to [IP Address] (unsafe)"
A small link will appear below the text saying "Proceed to 10.x.x.x (unsafe)" or "Continue to site". Click it.
3
Log In Normally
The UniFi Protect login screen will appear. Your browser will remember this choice so you won't need to do it every time.

* If your district pushes custom root certificates to staff laptops via Group Policy or Intune, this warning will not appear. Contact IT if you need the certificate installed on your device.
